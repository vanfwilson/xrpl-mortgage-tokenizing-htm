## Notes on the draft

- **Leads by acknowledging the closed window.** Pretending not to know applications are closed
  reads as a mass mailing. Naming it, and naming the October announcement, shows the homework and
  gives them a low-cost way to respond ("we'll flag you when it opens").
- **Puts the two verifiable links high.** A grants reviewer's first question is "does it exist."
  Running code and a published evidence report answer it before any claim has to be taken on
  trust.
- **Mirrors the program's own stated focus areas** (RWA, collateral movement, tokenization) in
  their language, without overclaiming — it is Testnet, and the letter says so.
- **Offers two exits, one of them cheap.** Asking only for a demo makes "no" the easy answer.
  Adding "or just point us at the right intake" makes a useful reply nearly free.
- **Keeps it to Testnet claims only.** No Mainnet or production-volume implication anywhere.
