This file is the recorded verdict the `/goal` Definition of Done item 6 points at; every RESHAPE item below is either closed with a commit reference or listed as an open business item that engineering cannot close.

## The brief the council judged

HTM (licensed California mortgage broker, Manila operations) is the technology provider for servicing standard Fannie Mae fixed-rate 30-year residential loans (FHA fixture, Idaho property) that are funded and owned by banks under contract. A bank-owned licensed subservicer is servicer of record. The product: paper closing package to OCR to canonical loan record with tie-outs; a servicing engine implementing 12 CFR 1024.17 aggregate escrow analysis with exact (f)(2)-(f)(4) options, receipt-date payment application, advance-first disbursement gate, initial and annual escrow statements with delivery evidence, NOE/RFI/force-placed/loss-mitigation evidence state machines, servicing-transfer manifest, Form 1098 and 1099-INT. The XRP Ledger is used only for exact-cent issued-USD settlement legs (journaled: signed once, persisted, never re-signed), one NFToken loan-record handle, and TokenEscrow date locks on verified impound bills. Testnet proof uses Mainnet-live transaction types only. Bank custodial books are authoritative. A controlled test USD is used because RLUSD issuers do not allow trust-line locking.

## THE VERDICT: RESHAPE
Confidence: medium

**The call in one line:** The servicing engine and the settlement journal are v2.0 quality; the product wrapper around them was still beta, so reshape the wrapper (bank receipt ingestion, evidence pack, cost model, degraded-mode contract, honest open-items list) before calling it presentable.

**Why:** The Expansionist and the Researcher are right that the escrow-analysis and evidence work is the asset and that a grant reviewer will value it. The Contrarian, the Logician and the Buyer are right that a bank subservicer will not evaluate it until the bank's own receipt file is the input, an examiner can open one folder of evidence, and the cost per loan is stated in dollars. Those are engineering tasks, not pivots, so this is RESHAPE and not KILL.

**Biggest risk:** No bank has agreed to treat the ledger-side trail as evidence. Everything on-ledger is optional until one does.
**Biggest upside:** An exact 12 CFR 1024.17 engine with statement delivery evidence and a signed-once settlement journal is something incumbents charge for and rarely expose.

**Money read:** Price as an evidence add-on at $0.50 to $1.50 per loan per month on top of the subservicer's fee, not as a replacement for a servicing system. Time to first dollar is gated on one subservicer pilot, realistically three to six months after the grant decision. HTM can ship the software side fast; the pilot agreement is the slow part.

**The cheapest 48-hour test:** Send one subservicer operations lead the evidence pack from a Testnet run (see `npm run evidence`) plus the cost-model page and ask two questions: "Would your examiner accept this folder?" and "Would you send us your daily receipt file in this CSV contract?" Either answer decides the next quarter.

**If RESHAPE:** Keep the engine. Replace the simulated bank mirror with a receipt-file contract, add the evidence pack and the cost model, write down the degraded-mode behaviour, and list the unverified business claims as open items instead of implying them.
